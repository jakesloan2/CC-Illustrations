Privacy policy

Short version: this site collects almost nothing, and sells nothing on to anyone.

Last updated: August 2026

Who runs this site

This site belongs to CC Illustrations (Charlie Cully), based in Northern Ireland. For anything in this policy, email hello@ccillustrations.com.

What is collected

Nothing at all, unless you choose to give it. There is no account to create and no cookie banner, because the site sets no tracking cookies of its own.

You share information only when you:

  • Email or message Charlie. Your name, contact details and whatever you write are used to answer you and to run the commission or order, and nothing else.
  • Join the release list. Your email address is used to tell you about the book release and print drops. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and leaving removes you from the list entirely.

Analytics

If website analytics are switched on, they record anonymous, aggregated information — which pages are visited, roughly which country the visit came from, which device type. None of it identifies you, and none of it is used for advertising.

Who sees your information

Charlie, and the services needed to run the site — the web host, and the email provider that sends the release list. Your details are never sold, rented or passed to advertisers.

How long it is kept

Enquiry emails are kept as long as needed for the work and normal business records. List subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal information held about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how it is used. Email the address above and it will be dealt with within one month. If you are unhappy with the response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The work is made for children, but the site is aimed at the adults who buy books, prints and commissions. Nobody under 16 should sign up to the release list without a parent or guardian's say-so.

Other sites

Links out to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and WhatsApp are governed by those companies' own privacy policies, not this one.

Copyright

All artwork on this site is by Charlie Cully and remains his. It may not be copied, reproduced, printed or used commercially without written permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear here with a new date at the top.

Charlie C

Children's book illustrator. Fineliner, marker, and far too many characters per page.

Charlie C is an illustrator working out of Northern Ireland under the name CC Illustrations. He draws dense, hand-built worlds — every one of them started with a black fineliner and finished in alcohol marker, with nothing generated, traced or filled in by a machine.

The work is built to be looked at twice. A single piece might hold sixty characters: a shark reading a bottle label, a submarine with a face, a small boy grinning through it all. Children find something new on the fourth read, which is exactly the point, and it is why his pages hold up as picture book spreads, as prints on a bedroom wall, and as a graphic on the front of a t-shirt.

His debut picture book, My Time Travel Adventure, arrives this year. Alongside it he takes commissioned originals, character design, apparel graphics and portrait pieces, and runs live drawing sessions in schools and libraries.
